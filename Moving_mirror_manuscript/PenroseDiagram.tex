\begin{itemize}
    \item Past timelike infinity: \iminus\\
            To get the coordinates of past timelike infinity, we consider a particle that has a finite value of the \coordz and has $-\infty$ for the value of \coordt. But there are two cases: \begin{enumerate}
     \item[(R)] $t = -\infty$ and $z = + const$
     \item[(L)] $t = -\infty$ and $z = - const$
 \end{enumerate}
The bullet headings (L) and (R) will be clear later on. This gives us the value of light cone coordinates as \begin{enumerate}
    \item[(R)]  $U~=~-\infty$, $V~=~-\infty$.
    \item[(L)]  $U~=~-\infty$, $V~=~-\infty$.
\end{enumerate}    
    \item Past null infinity: \scrim\\
        To get the coordinates of past null infinity, we consider a particle that is infinitely far away from the origin in both coordinates. There are two cases: \begin{enumerate}
     \item[(R)] $t = -\infty$ and $z = + \infty$
     \item[(L)] $t = -\infty$ and $z = - \infty$
 \end{enumerate}
This gives us the value of light cone coordinates as \begin{enumerate}
    \item[(R)]  $U~=~-\infty$, $V~=~finite$.
    \item[(L)]  $U~=~finite$, $V~=~-\infty$.
\end{enumerate} 
    \item Future null infinity: \scrip\\
            To get the coordinates of future null infinity, we consider a particle that is infinitely far away from the origin in both coordinates but in the future. There are two cases: \begin{enumerate}
     \item[(R)] $t = +\infty$ and $z = + \infty$
     \item[(L)] $t = +\infty$ and $z = - \infty$
 \end{enumerate}
This gives us the value of light cone coordinates as \begin{enumerate}
    \item[(R)]  $U~=~finite$, $V~=~\infty$.
    \item[(L)]  $U~=~\infty$, $V~=~finite$.
\end{enumerate} 
    \item Future timelike infinity: \iplus\\
            To get the coordinates of future timelike infinity, we consider a particle that is infinitely far away from the origin in both coordinates but in the future. There are two cases: \begin{enumerate}
     \item[(R)] $t = +\infty$ and $z = + finite$
     \item[(L)] $t = +\infty$ and $z = - finite$
 \end{enumerate}
This gives us the value of light cone coordinates as \begin{enumerate}
    \item[(R)]  $U~=~+\infty$, $V~=~+\infty$.
    \item[(L)]  $U~=~+\infty$, $V~=~+\infty$.
\end{enumerate}   
\end{itemize}
